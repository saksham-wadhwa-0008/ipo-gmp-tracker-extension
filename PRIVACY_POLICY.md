# Privacy Policy for IPO GMP Tracker & Advisory

**Last Updated:** September 24, 2026

## 1. Overview
IPO GMP Tracker & Advisory ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

## 2. No Personal Data Collection
The Extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell** any personal data, sensitive user information, or web browsing history.

- We do not use cookies, user accounts, authentication, or tracking pixels.
- We do not run any analytics, telemetry, or remote advertising scripts.
- All operations execute entirely on your local machine within your browser environment.

## 3. Local Storage Usage
The Extension utilizes Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) exclusively on your device for:
- Saving your user-configured minimum GMP percentage threshold (default: 15%).
- Storing your locally marked "Applied" IPO watchlist.
- Caching public day-by-day GMP movement tables from `ipowatch.in` to display offline historical charts.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## 4. Network Requests
The Extension communicates only with `https://ipowatch.in` to fetch publicly available Grey Market Premium (GMP) table updates. No user data, device identifiers, or tracking tokens are sent in these requests.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
We do not share, sell, transfer, or disclose any user data to third parties.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Extension does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.

## 8. Contact Information
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the project repository or contact the developer.
